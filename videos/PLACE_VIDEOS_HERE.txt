Place Nicholas's video clips in this folder.

Recommended filenames (referenced in index.html):

  showreel.mp4         — featured video at top of video section
  leak-fix.mp4         — thumbnail video 1
  heater-install.mp4   — thumbnail video 2
  drain-unblock.mp4    — thumbnail video 3
  bathroom-pipe.mp4    — thumbnail video 4

Keep each clip under 50 MB for fast loading on mobile.
Use MP4 (H.264) format for maximum browser compatibility.

Until you add real videos, the video thumbnails will still display
with their poster images — clicking them opens a video player modal
that will show "no source" until the MP4 files exist. This is fine
for the first deploy; just add the videos later.

See README.md in the project root for full instructions.
