About Me

Meet Nicholas — Your Trusted Plumber

I started apprenticing at 18 under my uncle, a respected plumber in Eastlands. Twelve years later, I've handled everything from a single dripping tap to a full re-pipe of a 4-bedroom home — and I still treat every job like it's in my own house.

I'm licensed by the Nairobi City County (NCC Plumbing Licence No. PL-0427), fully insured, and proud of clean workmanship. No shortcuts, no surprise fees, no leaving a mess behind.
